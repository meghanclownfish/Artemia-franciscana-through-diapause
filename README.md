# Artemia-franciscana-through-diapause

To investigate gene expression patterns during post-diapause development in the brine shrimp, _Artemia franciscana_, we assembled and annotated a transcriptome. The code to do so can be found in (insert_folder)

## RNAseq data

**RNA extraction:** Qiagen RNeasy Lipid Tissue mini kits \
**Library prep:** TruSeq Stranded mRNA Prep kit \
**Sequencing:**  Illumina Novaseq6000 platform 

### Time series

Data were collected at multiple time points throughout post-diapause development. 
- 0 hours (around 4 hours after dehydrated embryos were hydrated) 
- 6 hours
- 10 hours
- 12 hours
- 14 hours
- 24 hours
- 70 hours
